# RB Group Holdings Ltd - Public Information

### Who We Are

RB Group Holdings Ltd is a Uganda-based Intellectual Property Holding Company and R&D Factory.

We build decision intelligence technology for financial services, government, and platform businesses including mobility. We license this technology to one regulated partner per sector per country per defined position. Partners operate. We own the IP.

We provide intelligence. You retain decision authority, execution and accountability.

- No customer funds handling
- No custody, no escrow, no settlement touch
- No customer operations

### Licensing Model

One Country. One Sector. One Defined Position.

Exclusivity is a contractual restriction on licensing a defined competitive position to another client. It is not blanket ownership of the underlying technology.

Example: Mobility Uganda Only — one client can deploy for Mobility in Uganda for the defined use-case. We can still license other sectors in Uganda to other clients.

Detailed commercial terms are disclosed in the Data Room under NDA.

### Technology Available for Licensing

Engine 1 - Intelligence Licence - Core: Churn, fraud and promo abuse signals, demand patterns, behavior signals, geographic intelligence, prioritisation and retention evidence.

Engine 2 - Retail Media - Optional: Advertiser demand intelligence and revenue attribution.

Each outcome delivers audit reports, risk scores, settlement reporting, benchmarks and one-pager reporting by zone and category.

### Governance + Data

- Royalty from day one with minimums per defined position
- All outputs advisory with human oversight — no autonomous financial decisions
